Maasai Mara - Hyenas

22 photos

In August 2026 I had the opportunity to go to the Maasai Mara National Reserve in Kenya. I was hosted by the Mara Hyena Project, which has been observing Spotted Hyenas in this region since 1988. I finally got to see the hyenas that I am studying and the environment that they live in. I also had a great time staying in the research camps and joining the research assistants on their daily observation drives.
